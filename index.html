Dynamic and Autonomous Robotic Systems (DARos) Lab

Graduate Research Assistant

Joined the DARos Lab.

The team is currently developing a robotic guide dog designed for providing navigational assistance for the visually impaired.
I'm responsible for the vision component of the dog which involves being able to understand vehicles, pedestrians, traffic lights, crosswalks, walk signs, tactile pavings, etc. This then integrates with path-planning and language system in order to make a full conversational assistant for a user!

We recently had a demo testing various features out, you can check it out here!
Our work was accepted at the CVPR 2025 AVA workshop

Currently working on incorporating neuromorphic devices such as event cameras into the vision pipeline for low-latency and low-power consumption. We're aiming to build a complete foundational model for event-based vision for assistive robotics!

Research Projects

Neuro-eye

Neuromorphic Vision Sensor for high speed event-based object detection with low power consumption inspired by the human retina and visual cortex. Electronic circuit encodes photon events into spike trains and processed using spiking neural networks.

Upcoming

Bionic Reflex

Intelligent reflex arc module for the neuromuscular system of prosthetic legs and exoskeletons. Capable of generating stabilizing reflexive motor torques and muscles activations in response to unexpected perturbations during locomotion.
